## 📖 Description
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of BMW’s global model performance, analyzing units sold, pricing trends, engine-type distribution, and profitability across regions. It equips business decision-makers with actionable insights to optimize product distribution, pricing strategies, and investment forecasting.

By leveraging advanced DAX measures, interactive visuals, and drillthrough pages, this dashboard transitions from static reporting to a dynamic intelligence solution that empowers regional alignment and executive decision-making.

## 🧠 DAX Measures
```DAX
Total Revenue = SUM('Sales Data'[Revenue])
Units Sold = SUM('Sales Data'[Units Sold])
Avg Price = AVERAGE('Sales Data'[Price])
Model Count = DISTINCTCOUNT('Models'[Model Name])
Profit Margin Category = IF([Profit %] > 20, "High", IF([Profit %] > 10, "Moderate", "Low"))
```
These calculated fields enabled performance segmentation, custom visuals, and automated KPI highlights.

## ❓ Business Questions Answered

- Which BMW models generate the most global revenue and units sold?
- What engine types perform best by region?
- Which price brackets dominate sales in different regions?
- How can product distribution be optimized geographically?
- What customer behaviors and market trends drive profit margins?

## 📌 Insights and Observations

- **Europe** ranks #1 in both revenue and unit sales.
- **BMW X5 and 3 Series** are consistently top-performing.
- **EV adoption** leads in Germany and China.
- **High-margin units** dominate North American sales.
- **Petrol SUVs** still prevail in mid-income markets.

---

## 📉 Visual Features

- 📊 **Bar Charts** – Sales and revenue by region and model
- 📋 **Matrix Table** – Profit margin by engine type and country
- 🔁 **Drillthrough Pages** – Deep dive into each model’s history
- 🧭 **Slicers** – Filter by model type, country, series, engine, and year
- 💡 **KPI Cards** – Top revenue series, model count, average unit cost

---

## 💡 Actionable Insights

1. **Expand EV models** in countries showing fast EV adoption trends.
2. **Bundle mid-tier models** (e.g., 5 Series) with seasonal offers in emerging regions.
3. **Reduce inventory** in overstocked markets with low sales conversion.
4. **Use historical trends** to plan new model launches by continent.
